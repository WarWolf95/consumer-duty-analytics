## Executive Summary

This executive briefing presents the quarterly performance and regulatory outcome analysis for the firm’s retail product portfolio under the **FCA Consumer Duty (PS22/9)** mandate.

Across our 50,000 active customer profiles and 22 core product lines, the portfolio exhibits strong overall commercial growth but highlights critical operational friction and fair value risks requiring board-level remediation.

## 1. Products & Services Outcome

**Regulatory Requirement**: Products must be fit for purpose, designed for target customer needs, and distributed through appropriate channels.

### Key Observations
* **Distribution Concentration**: Direct digital channels represent 62% of sales, while Broker channels account for 28% and Tied Agents 10%.
* **Product Target Market Match**: Pure protection and home insurance lines demonstrate high retention (>92%), indicating strong target market alignment.
* **Mis-selling Risk Flag**: Travel Broker distribution shows an elevated early cancellation rate (12.4%), signaling potential friction at the point of sale.

---

## 2. Price & Value Outcome (Fair Value Assessment)

**Regulatory Requirement**: Products must deliver fair value where the total price paid by the customer is reasonable relative to the benefits provided.

### Key Observations & FCA Benchmark Findings
1. **Motor Insurance Value Risk**:
   * **Firm Claims Ratio**: 40.4% vs **FCA Benchmark**: 54.4%.
   * **Assessment**: Incurred claims payouts are significantly lower than FCA industry standards, creating a potential Fair Value breach signal under FG22/5.
2. **Broker Commission Drag**:
   * **Travel Broker Product**: Commission ratio stands at **31.5%** of written premium, while claims acceptance rate drops to **72.1%**.
   * **Assessment**: High intermediary commission paired with lower claims acceptance represents value drag for consumers.

---

## 3. Consumer Support Outcome

**Regulatory Requirement**: Customers must not face unreasonable barriers to service, post-sale support, or complaint resolution.

### Key Observations
* **Overall SLA Compliance**: 71.2% of complaints resolved within 5 business days.
* **Root Cause Breakdown**:
  1. *Admin & Process Errors*: 38% of complaint volume.
  2. *Unreasonable Service Barriers*: 27% of complaint volume (phone queues and digital portal friction).
  3. *Claims Disputes*: 22% of complaint volume.
* **Financial Redress**: Cumulative redress paid across the portfolio reached **£142,500** for the reporting period.

---

## 4. Vulnerability Outcome Disparity (Cross-Cutting Focus)

**Regulatory Requirement**: Vulnerable consumers must receive fair treatment and outcomes equivalent to non-vulnerable consumers.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            VULNERABILITY DISPARITY GAP                            |
|                                                                                   |
| Non-Vulnerable Avg Resolution:  4.6 Days  [==============>                      ] |
| Vulnerable Cohort Avg Resolution: 6.7 Days  [=================================> ] |
| Disparity Gap:                    +2.1 Days (35% Slower Resolution)              |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Key Observations
* **Resolution Speed Disparity**: Vulnerable customers experience an average resolution time of **6.7 days** compared to **4.6 days** for non-vulnerable customers (+2.1 days gap).
* **SLA Compliance Gap**: Complaint SLA compliance for vulnerable customers is **55.2%** vs **71.8%** for non-vulnerable customers across all product categories.
* **Vulnerability Drivers**:
  * *Health*: 32% of vulnerable cohort (complex claims requirements cause longest delays).
  * *Financial Resilience*: 28% of vulnerable cohort.
  * *Life Events*: 22% of vulnerable cohort.
  * *Capability*: 18% of vulnerable cohort.

---

## Strategic Action Plan & Recommendations

1. **Vulnerability Service Taskforce (Immediate Priority)**:
   * Establish a dedicated vulnerable customer support desk to reduce complaint resolution time from 6.7 days to < 5.0 days, closing the +2.1 day gap.
2. **Motor Insurance Pricing & Value Review**:
   * Conduct a Fair Value Assessment on Motor Insurance to adjust pricing or enhance policy coverage, bringing claims ratio closer to the 54.4% FCA benchmark.
3. **Intermediary Distribution Audit**:
   * Review commission caps on Travel Broker distribution to eliminate price distortion and value drag.
4. **Governance Oversight**:
   * Present updated disparity metrics to the Board Consumer Duty Champion on a monthly basis.
